Every learned step, imputation, encoding and scaling, sits inside one scikit-learn pipeline fitted on the training fold only and cloned for each model, so no validation or test statistic reaches a model. Missing numbers get the fold median and an indicator; skewed amounts get a log. We keep the late tail, because very late orders are real customers, and model the log of lead time instead. The newest 20% of orders were held out first and read once.
