0:35. Each prediction is made when someone can act on it. Lead time is predicted at checkout, when fulfilment shows the customer a delivery range, and we judge it by mean absolute error in days. The review alert is for customer service. Detractors are a minority, {{B9.clf.test_prevalence_B}} of test orders, so accuracy would mislead and we use average precision. We weight a missed detractor as five false alerts, which puts the alert threshold at one in six.
