0:40. Lead time is predicted at checkout, the moment a promise is shown. The alert is predicted at carrier handover: at checkout nothing about this order's dispatch is known, and late delivery is the strongest driver of a bad review. Detractors are a minority, so accuracy would mislead; average precision is judged against the detractor share. The 5:1 cost turns the ranking into a decision: alert when the risk is above 1/6.
