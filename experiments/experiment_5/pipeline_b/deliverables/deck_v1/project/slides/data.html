0:50. Items, payments and reviews have several rows per order, so each is aggregated to the order before the join; that keeps the join one to one. A column may be a predictor only if its value exists at the prediction moment. At checkout that excludes every delivery, carrier and review field. At handover the improved alert may use this order's dispatch delay, but still not its delivery or review. History features use only other orders whose events were complete in earlier weeks.
