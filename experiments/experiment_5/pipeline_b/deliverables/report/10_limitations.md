## 10 Limitations

Table K17 lists every remaining limit and how it is handled. The ones that matter most for reading the results: Pipeline B covers only the seven states and alerts at a later moment than Pipeline A; the operating threshold 4/15 was chosen after the test, so the frozen 1/6 is the unbiased result; the test period was faster than development; validation scores are slightly optimistic and the alert's feature ranks unstable; three deviations are recorded (section 6); every finding is an association, not a cause; and section 9's business case is a post-test view in which the effect of a contact is unmeasured.
