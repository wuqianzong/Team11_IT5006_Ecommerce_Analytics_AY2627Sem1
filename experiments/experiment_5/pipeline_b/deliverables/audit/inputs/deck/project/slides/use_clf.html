0:30. How customer service uses the alert. Pipeline B at four in fifteen sends 5.6 alerts a day and about 1.9 of them are real detractors. A's fallback adds 14.9 a day at lower precision, and on the test those alerts cost more than none, so a higher fallback threshold is an option to test. The missed-dispatch rule works inside the seven states, beside B. In the proposed workflow, customer service sets the threshold, works the daily queue and reads the case file in the app, and the reason picks the action: a new date and an apology after a late dispatch, expediting on a slow route, a refund or voucher when freight is high. The contact happens outside the app, and each week the list goes to seller management. Freight discounts move few alerts, and that is an association. The next step is a randomised pilot.
