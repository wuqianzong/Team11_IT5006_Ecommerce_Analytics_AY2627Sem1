0:30. How customer service uses the alert. Pipeline B at four in fifteen sends 5.6 alerts a day and about 1.9 of them are real detractors. A's fallback adds 14.9 a day at lower precision, so a higher fallback threshold or the missed-dispatch rule alone are options. The app runs the workflow: set the threshold, work the daily queue, open the case file, call and offer a voucher, and pass sellers to seller management weekly. Freight discounts move few alerts, and that is an association. The next step is a randomised pilot.
