Team 11 · IT5006 · Dual framing of one problem, as the brief allows

We frame one problem twice. The regression gives the delivery promise at checkout. The classifier flags likely detractors when the parcel is handed to the carrier, because late delivery drives bad reviews and the seller’s delay is only known then. Imbalance: we use average precision, not accuracy.
